**Commit messages.** A subject and at most two sentences of body, carrying only what the diff cannot show: a non-obvious constraint, a deliberate trade-off. Never restate the diff, list files or hunks, record measurements, or lean on a journal. Origin narration (how the change came to be, what prompted it, which pass or session surfaced it) is not message content. The body follows `writing-style` for how it reads. Format is the repo's call; where the log shows no format of its own, conventional commits (`type(scope):`) are the default. A sentence the diff contradicts is a defect; so is framing that presumes a history the tree lacks ("replaces X", "removes the legacy Y").
